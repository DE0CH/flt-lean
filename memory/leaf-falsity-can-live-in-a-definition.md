`exists_traceGenerated_of_isWeaklyUniversal` (`Modularity/Patching.lean`) looked
like a pure "hard theorem" leaf (Carayol descent). It was in fact **unprovable
as stated**, and the defect was not in its signature at all — it was in
`IsTraceGeneratedDeformation`, a definition written two days earlier and used
only as a hypothesis in ten other statements.

**The technique that found it: push the CONCLUSION down onto the ambient data.**
The conclusion's clauses do not live in isolation — they sit in one existential
telescope and can be combined. Here `IsAdic (maximalIdeal Runiv)` +
`Function.Surjective πuniv` make `πuniv` continuous into a discrete finite field;
applying it to the trace-generation clause and the trace-reduction clause in the
same telescope yields `k = 𝔽_p(traces of ρbar)` — a statement about `(k, ρbar)`
alone that no hypothesis supplies and that fails after any finite extension of
the coefficient field. Ask of any resistant leaf: *what does the conclusion say
about data the hypotheses do not constrain?*

**The mathematical trap it came from, worth remembering on its own:** Carayol /
Mazur trace-generation of a deformation ring is over the **coefficient ring
`W(k)`**, never over `ℤ_[p]`. The two agree only when `k = 𝔽_p`. Transcribing
"topologically generated by traces" as `Algebra.adjoin ℤ_[p]` silently demands
the residue field be generated by traces. The Cohen-free way to state the real
thing: *every `ℤ_[p]`-subalgebra containing the traces and surjecting onto the
residue field is dense*. That still kills the `R₀[[y]]` inflation family the
clause exists to exclude (the constants are such a subalgebra and are closed),
and it does not force `k = 𝔽_p`.

**A definition nothing consumes accumulates MORE than one recorded defect.**
The same predicate separately owned the `Sf \ Suniv` gap, recorded twice in a
consumer's docstring as if it were that consumer's problem: the clause spoke
about traces away from `Suniv` while the consumer only ever gets traces away
from a larger, uncontrolled `Sf ∪ Suniv`. Both defects were fixable in ONE
arity-preserving edit. So when you repair an unconsumed definition, `grep` its
name across the module first and collect every complaint about it — they are
cheaper together than in sequence, and CLAUDE.md's "two individually-correct
repairs can be fatal together" is then a single composite audit rather than two.

**Why the repair was cheap, and how to check that in advance:** `grep` the
hypothesis name. `hgen` appeared ten times and was CONSUMED BY NO PROOF BODY —
every occurrence was a binder or a forwarding application down to a still-sorried
node. A definition nothing computes with can be restated for free; the build was
byte-identical in job count. See [[flt-two-leaves-may-be-one]] and
[[audit-searched-production-not-invariant]] for the sibling techniques.
