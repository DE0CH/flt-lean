## A DOCSTRING'S PROPOSED GLUE IS UNCHECKED MATHEMATICS — INSTANTIATE IT ON THE INTENDED CASE
(2026-08-01, `flt-lean-320`, `Modularity/TateModule.lean`.) A mature leaf here often ends with
a paragraph headed *"THE SMALLEST KNOWN SUFFICIENT INPUT"* or *"the glue is then immediate"*,
naming a classical theorem and a two-line derivation of the leaf from it. That paragraph is
the single most load-bearing thing in the docstring — it is what a successor's whole plan is
built on — and it is the ONE part that no audit checks, because it is about the *proof* and
every audit in this development is about the *statement*.
`exists_boundedLevelScalar_atPrime_finiteBase`'s read:
> If the `ℤ`-module `M` generated by `𝒪_D` and `Fr + V` inside `End_k(A')` is FINITELY
> GENERATED, globality is immediate: `Fr + V ∈ ⋂ₙ (L + Iⁿ M)` with `L` the image of `𝒪_D`,
> and `⋂ₙ qⁿ G = 0` for every finitely generated abelian group `G` gives `Fr + V ∈ L`.
Both halves are false. `⋂ₙ qⁿ G = 0` holds for TORSION-FREE `G` only — the intersection
stabilises at the prime-to-`q` torsion, and `G = ℤ/5`, `q = 7` refutes it outright. And `M/L`
is exactly a torsion module in the intended case: the leaf's own `htower` forces real
multiplication by `D` of degree `dim A'`, so for `A'` simple `D = ℚ(π)⁺` CONTAINS
`Fr + V = π + π̄`; approached through a rational relation `d·(Fr + V) = c` with `d ∉ I` one has
`M/L ≅ 𝒪_D/(d)` and `I + (d) = 𝒪_D`, hence `Iⁿ(M/L) = M/L` and `⋂ₙ (L + Iⁿ M) = M`. **Krull's
intersection theorem is vacuous on precisely the quotient the argument applies it to.**
**The check is one line and it is not "is this theorem true": INSTANTIATE THE GLUE AT THE CASE
THE LEAF IS ABOUT.** A Krull/Nakayama/Artin–Rees step is a statement about a QUOTIENT module,
and the interesting cases are exactly the ones where that quotient degenerates — which is why
the abstract argument reads fine and the instance does not. Ask, for the module the step is
applied to: is it torsion-free? finitely generated? nonzero? If the leaf's own hypotheses force
the degenerate answer, the glue is decoration.
**And the repair usually names the residue better than the original did.** What actually closes
Weil's rationality is INTEGRAL CLOSEDNESS, not Krull: `c/d ∈ D` integral over `ℤ` lies in `𝒪_D`.
So the honest residue is a CONJUNCTION — the limit is *rational* (`d·sₙ ≡ c`, `d ∉ I`) and
*integral* (`P(sₙ) ≡ 0` for one monic `P ∈ ℤ[X]`) — and neither clause implies the other:
`D = ℚ`, `I = (5)`, `d = 3`, `c = 1` is rational and not integral (`1/3 ∉ ℤ`); `I = (7)`,
`P = X² − 2` is integral and not rational. Both counterexamples are three symbols long and
neither is findable from the abstract statement.
Two riders worth copying:
* **Krull is still the right tool one level down.** `⋂ₙ Iⁿ = 0` in the RING `𝒪_D`
  (`Ideal.iInf_pow_eq_bot_of_isDomain`) is unconditional and is what turns "≡ 0 at every level"
  into "= 0". Use it there, not on a quotient module you have not inspected.
* **The whole glue was ~120 lines of pure commutative algebra and verified in FIVE SECONDS**
  against a mathlib-only scratch, then in seven against the target's own olean — while one
  `lake build` of the 24 800-line target is ~20 minutes. When a leaf's residue is
  ring-theoretic, develop it with NO project import at all and transplant; the project-facing
  half is then only the statement, which a scratch importing the target's olean checks in one
  more round.
