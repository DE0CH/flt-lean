## A CONSUMERLESS OPEN LEAF IS A DELETION, AND THE COUNT MOVES ONLY IF YOU DELETE IT
(Same task.) `redX_base_ne_and_card_compl_range_le_of_jNeronDatum` was a merged
two-conjunct leaf. On 2026-07-31 a fifth cut reproved the consumer of its FIRST
conjunct over a new structure; on 2026-08-01 the consumer of its SECOND was
reproved the same way. At that point a comment-stripped grep for its name returned
its own declaration line and prose, and nothing else — the seventh invisibility
class, reached in two steps by two agents neither of whom left it consumerless on
purpose.
**The check is one grep per leaf you supersede, and it must be run at the moment
you supersede it**, not later: the first agent could not have run it (the second
conjunct still had a consumer), and the second only ran it because the first had
written "the counting sibling is untouched" into a section docstring. **When you
reprove one consumer of a multi-conjunct leaf, say in the docstring which
conjuncts are now orphaned** — that sentence is what makes the deletion findable.
Two riders on doing the deletion:
* **the frontier moves only on the deletion**, not on the reproof. Reproving the
  second consumer is `−0`; deleting the leaf is `−1`. Report them as one step or
  the arithmetic reads as unexplained;
* **keep the deleted docstring's ANALYSIS, and say where each piece went.** That
  leaf carried a derivability audit with two junk-shape counterexamples
  (`Spec R[t]/(t²−q)` for separation, `Spec (R × 𝔽_q)` for counting) which are
  precisely what its replacement structure excludes; a deletion that drops them
  loses the only record of why the structure has the fields it has.
**And check whether the deleted statement is merely UNPROVEN or actually
UNPROVABLE-FROM-THE-REPLACEMENT.** Here the second conjunct compared the two
fibres of the cuspidal subscheme, which is FALSE without the squarefreeness
hypothesis (`ℤ_(5)[t]/(t²+1)` is finite étale with one generic and two special
points) — and that hypothesis cannot be put on the replacement structure, because
a sibling consumes the same structure without it. So the conjunct is not a
statement somebody should re-open; say so, or it will be.
